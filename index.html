Approach

Dream Scenario is a research-led strategy and design studio.

We help organizations understand how behavior and context shape customer decisions, then help them close the gap between business goals and what customers need.

We think design is meant to create value for the people it touches, and that organizations who share that conviction get better outcomes for their business. This studio is built around that mission.

People come to us when complex, ambiguous challenges have become overwhelming, or the human side of the problem isn't clear. We work at whatever scale the problem calls for, from focused sessions to multi-phase programs.

We are independent and technology agnostic to focus on creating the best experience.

Human-Centered Strategy

Ensure organizations solve the right problems before scaling by mapping human insights onto concrete business opportunities and strategic goals.

This reduces uncertainty by defining what to do and why, so downstream initiatives have clear purpose, utility, and efficacy.

Common Deliverables
  • Ethnographic research
  • Behavior & mindset analysis
  • Jobs-to-be-done research
  • Customer/user archetypes
  • Insight synthesis
  • Opportunity mapping
  • Journey maps
  • Participatory design research
  • Concept-led research
  • Usability & efficacy testing

Service Design

Orchestrate how a service should work by connecting every human, digital, physical, and operational element.

This goes beyond CX or UX to deliver a coherent, buildable vision grounded in both human needs and how the organization works.

Common Deliverables
  • Service blueprints
  • Customer & ecosystem maps
  • Current state / future state analyses
  • Experience visions & principles
  • System & process flows
  • Service prototypes
  • Experience roadmaps

Spatial & Digital Experiences

Design for physical, digital, or hybrid environments by harmonizing space, workflow, and the environmental factors shaping how people navigate space and time.

This reduces friction, provides tangible benefits, and strengthens the relationship between people and the brand.

Common Deliverables
  • Workflow & setting analysis
  • Experience concepts and prototypes
  • Workspace optimization
  • Systems integration recommendations

Behavioral Insights

Investigate the cognitive, environmental, and social factors driving people's choices to untangle why they behave the way they do.

This connects root behaviors to business goals and proves especially valuable in complex, high-stakes programs where understanding behavioral context is critical.

Common Deliverables
  • Behavioral research and analysis
  • Decision context mapping
  • Barrier and motivator identification
  • Behavior change frameworks

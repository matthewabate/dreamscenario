Approach

Dream Scenario is a research-led strategy and design studio.

We help organizations understand how behavior and context shape customer decisions, then help them close the gap between business goals and what customers need.

We think design is meant to create value for the people it touches, and that organizations who share that conviction get better outcomes for their business. This studio is built around that mission.

People come to us when complex, ambiguous challenges have become overwhelming, or the human side of the problem isn't clear. We work at whatever scale the problem calls for, from focused sessions to multi-phase programs.

We are medium, environment, and technology agnostic to focus on creating the best experience, and independent to prioritize our clients and their audiences.

Strategic Design

Identify a purpose-led path to your targets.

Target-setting and market alignment are made credible through untangling what customers need and defining a strategy with a realistic chain of events.

Common Challenges
  • Is a problem rooted in product, process, or people?
  • What customers say and what they do don't match.
  • Is demand for a new offering real?
  • Get teams and leadership aligned on the problem we're actually solving.
  • Is there a new market for our business goals?
  • Turn more guests into repeat visitors.

Experience Architecture

Orchestrate the elements & connections of an experience.

Structuring complexity across environments into something intuitive to smooth customer journeys, organize teamwork, and build systems that last.

Common Challenges
  • Our touchpoints don't function as one experience.
  • Why are people dropping off between handoffs?
  • Our systems create a fragmented journey.
  • Our website doesn't feed our CRM very well.
  • The data in our systems isn't informing our service improvement process.
  • Our workflow is holding back our customer experience.

Experience Design

Make complex things intuitive & memorable.

Defining the form of a great experience, with a differentiated character that matters and sits comfortably in the organization's product, brand, and operational reality.

Common Challenges
  • Make a valuable archive or body of knowledge usable.
  • Why isn't our product performing the way we expected?
  • How do we make our touchpoints more compelling?
